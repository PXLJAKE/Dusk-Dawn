# Changelog

Every version is published automatically as a [GitHub release](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/releases), and HACS then offers it as an update. The full release notes with all pull requests are there. Here is a summary.

## 0.3.9

- Uploading own recordings no longer blocks Home Assistant, oversized uploads are cleaned up ([#31](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/31)).

## 0.3.8

- Alarm tone "bird chorus" removed. Bells and Classic remain ([#30](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/30)).

## 0.3.7

- The sidebar shows its real version in the browser console ([#29](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/29)).

## 0.3.6

- Scenes: own scenes with light and colour gradient, reordering by drag and drop, "Good night" as an editable suggestion ([#28](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/28)).
- Sound lamp: no more stuttering with many sounds ([#27](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/27)).

## 0.3.5

- Sounds and alarm much quieter: slider at the top −18 dB, alarm another −12 dB ([#26](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/26)). The sound lamp needs a software update for this.
- Docs: integration and sound lamp separated ([#25](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/25)).

## 0.3.4

- Own sounds unlimited and nameable. The built-in nature sounds (including brook) were removed ([#24](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/24)).

## 0.3.3

- Quieter sounds and alarm, presence start only after 10 minutes, automation by time window only, a switched-off sleep timer stays off ([#23](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/23)).

## 0.3.2

- Sound lamp: the alarm runs on the lamp itself, even without Home Assistant ([#22](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/22)).

## 0.3.1

- New logo ([#21](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/21)).

## 0.3.0

- Upload own recordings, played seamlessly ([#19](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/19)).
- Automatic release on every merge so that HACS offers updates ([#20](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/20)).
- Sound lamp: optional EQ, corrected speaker hole pattern ([#17](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/17), [#18](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/pull/18)).

## Before 0.3.0

There were no versioned releases before 0.3.0. Earlier development is in the [commit history](https://github.com/PXLJAKE/Dusk-Dawn/commits/main).
